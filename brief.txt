BELTAL — PROJECT BRIEF
Blockchain-Enabled Trusted Access & Asset Ledger
Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Problem Statement 26125 · Bharat Electronics Limited


================================================================================
1. THE PROBLEM
================================================================================

BEL runs nine manufacturing units and several Strategic Business Units working on
classified defence electronics — military radars, electronic warfare, military
communications, cyber systems. Three things have to be tracked continuously:

  - who someone is and what security clearance they hold,
  - which physical labs and zones they are allowed to walk into,
  - who is currently holding each piece of expensive, classified equipment.

Today all three live in ordinary centralized databases: Active Directory for
identity, a badge-reader system for door access, and departmental spreadsheets
and paper gate passes for equipment custody.

The weakness is not that these systems are missing features. It is that every one
of them can be edited quietly by anyone with database privileges, and the edit
leaves no trace. A privileged insider can raise their own clearance level, grant
themselves access to a crypto lab, or rewrite who signed out a spectrum analyser
— and the record will look perfectly normal afterwards. When auditors from the
MoD, DGQA or CERT-In come to inspect, they are shown rows from exactly the
database that could have been altered before they arrived. They have no
independent way to tell a genuine record from a doctored one.

So the real problem is not access control. It is that there is no way to prove
that the access-control records are telling the truth.


================================================================================
2. THE APPROACH
================================================================================

BELTAL does not replace BEL's HR system, badge readers or ERP. It adds a
cryptographic layer underneath them that makes every identity change, access
decision and custody transfer permanently and independently verifiable.

The core design rule is:

    THE BLOCKCHAIN IS THE SOURCE OF TRUTH.
    THE DATABASE IS A DISPOSABLE CACHE.

Every write — registering a person, granting a role, minting an asset,
transferring custody, locking down a zone — goes to a smart contract first. The
contracts emit events; a background indexer copies those events into PostgreSQL
purely so dashboards load quickly. Nothing is ever written to the database as a
primary record.

That inversion is what defeats the insider. Editing the database no longer
changes reality; it only makes the cache disagree with the chain. And BELTAL
ships a verifier whose entire job is to find that disagreement and report it.

Personal data never goes on-chain. Only a DID and a salted cryptographic hash are
recorded publicly. Full personnel dossiers and asset specifications are encrypted
with AES-256-GCM before they leave the server and pinned to IPFS; only the
content address is stored on-chain.


================================================================================
3. HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

FOUR SMART CONTRACTS (Solidity, deployed to Ethereum Sepolia)

  IdentityRegistry  DIDs, identity hashes, clearance levels, revocation, and
                    batch registration for bulk HR onboarding.
  AccessControl     Role grants, facility zones, temporary cross-SBU passes,
                    emergency lockdown, and the on-chain access decision.
  AssetNFT          Soulbound custody tokens. Deliberately NOT an ERC-721:
                    ordinary transfers revert. Custody can only move through
                    the controlled flow, and the contract itself refuses to
                    hand a classified asset to someone whose clearance is too
                    low or whose identity is not active.
  AuditLog          Append-only event stream written to by the other three.

BACKEND (Node.js / Express, PostgreSQL via Prisma, ethers.js)

  Submits every write to the contracts, enforces roles on every route, indexes
  chain events into the read-cache, encrypts and pins documents to IPFS, and
  runs the anti-tamper verifier.

FRONTEND (React + Vite + Tailwind)

  Four role-specific portals plus the verification and simulator tools. Sign-in
  is by wallet signature — no passwords.


THE FLOW, END TO END

  1. A person connects their wallet. The server issues a one-time nonce, the
     wallet signs it, the server verifies the signature and issues a JWT. A
     wallet with no identity gets a limited session that can do nothing except
     submit a registration request.

  2. An admin approves the request, choosing a role and clearance level. That
     approval mints a DID, encrypts the dossier to IPFS, writes the DID and
     identity hash on-chain, and grants the role in the contract.

  3. Equipment is minted as a soulbound token naming its custodian, its
     classification tier and its SBU.

  4. Custody moves only through request → approve → execute. The backend checks
     the recipient's clearance and SBU, the contract checks them again, and two
     separate people are always required: the requester cannot approve their own
     request, and an approver cannot be the recipient.

  5. A badge tap at a door is submitted by a machine identity. The decision is
     computed on-chain from clearance, SBU, any active cross-SBU pass and the
     zone's lockdown state — then logged. An admin can lock a zone instantly and
     it denies everyone.

  6. Every one of those events lands in the on-chain audit log and is indexed for
     fast querying.


THE PART THAT ANSWERS THE PROBLEM STATEMENT

  An auditor opens the verification page and pastes in an employee ID, an asset
  ID or a transaction hash. The system then:

    - fetches the encrypted dossier from IPFS and decrypts it,
    - recomputes the identity hash from the original fields,
    - reads the hash the blockchain actually holds,
    - reads the asset's custodian and CID from the chain,
    - and compares all of it against what the database claims.

  If they match, the record is provably genuine. If they do not, the response is
  INTEGRITY_COMPROMISED — the database was edited behind the application's back.

  This is the heart of the submission. The auditor is not being asked to trust
  BELTAL's word. They are being shown a mathematical comparison against a ledger
  nobody at BEL can rewrite.


================================================================================
4. ROLES
================================================================================

  ADMIN              Approves registrations, assigns roles and clearance, mints
                     assets, manages zones, triggers emergency lockdown, full
                     audit access.
  MANAGER            Sees and acts within their own SBU: team roster, team
                     assets, transfer approvals, cross-SBU passes.
  AUDITOR            Read-only across everything, plus the verification tools.
                     Cannot change a single record — by design.
  USER               Sees their own identity and the equipment in their custody,
                     and can request a transfer.
  SYSTEM_CONNECTOR   Machine-only identity for automated badge-reader and HR
                     feeds. Rejected on human sign-in.

Roles always come from the server, never from the client. The role picker on the
login screen is cosmetic — users are routed to the portal for their real role.
Clearance levels run 1 (Restricted) to 4 (Top Secret) and gate both door access
and asset custody.


================================================================================
5. WHAT IS WORKING
================================================================================

Smart contracts    All four implemented and deployed to Sepolia, with a test
                   suite covering role gating, clearance and SBU rules, the
                   soulbound property and hash verification.

Backend            Wallet sign-in, self-registration and approval, identity and
                   role management, asset minting, the full transfer flow,
                   cross-SBU passes, PACS zone access and lockdown, the event
                   indexer, the audit API and the anti-tamper verifier.
                   Also: bulk CSV onboarding, guardian account recovery, an
                   IPFS fetch-and-decrypt endpoint, and the AI audit assistant.

Frontend           Landing and login, self-registration, all four role
                   dashboards, identity ledger, role assignment, asset minting
                   and registry, transfer approvals, team views, the audit trail
                   explorer, the verification page with its Anti-Tamper Lab, the
                   PACS badge-tap simulator, a shared asset-detail view with an
                   ownership-history timeline, and the AI assistant panel.
                   Responsive down to phone width.

Demo data          One command seeds identities for every role across two SBUs,
                   zones, assets, a pending transfer, a cross-SBU pass and a
                   pending registration. Idempotent, and runnable fully offline
                   with --offchain for a laptop demo with no testnet ETH.

Three features worth calling out:

  BULK ONBOARDING — an admin uploads an HR CSV export. Every row is validated
  and the generated DIDs previewed BEFORE any transaction is sent; only if the
  whole file is clean are identities registered on-chain in batches of up to 250
  per transaction. A bad file is never half-imported.

  GUARDIAN RECOVERY — if someone loses the wallet their identity is anchored to,
  guardians assigned to them vouch for a replacement. Once enough have approved
  — and never on the word of whoever raised the request — an admin re-links the
  identity: the old wallet is revoked on-chain and the same DID and identity hash
  are re-anchored to the new one. The audit trail shows one person changing keys,
  not a new employee appearing from nowhere.

  AI AUDIT ASSISTANT — auditors can ask plain-English questions ("show me every
  denied badge tap last week"). The assistant can only reach the audit trail
  through the same role-gated service the REST API uses, so it can never surface
  a record the person asking could not already read. Crucially, the answer is
  displayed alongside the actual records it was built from — so the summary can
  be checked, not just trusted. Optional: disabled unless an API key is set.


================================================================================
6. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

Stated plainly, because a security project that hides its gaps is not a security
project.

  - The `signature` argument on custody transfers is stored on-chain as an
    opaque memo but is not cryptographically verified by the contract yet.
    Assigned to the blockchain sub-team.

  - SBU confinement is enforced in AccessControl (door access) and in the
    backend transfer checks, but not inside AssetNFT's custody path itself.

  - JWTs live one hour and are not re-checked against the database, so a role
    change or revocation takes effect at most an hour later.

  - Bulk import and guardian recovery are complete and usable through the API
    but do not yet have dedicated admin screens.

  - A few secondary frontend routes are still "Coming Soon" placeholders (audit
    vault, DID issuance, sovereign nodes, read-only ledgers).

  - Running on Sepolia testnet for the demo, not a production permissioned
    network.


================================================================================
7. IN ONE PARAGRAPH
================================================================================

BEL's identity, access and asset records are trustworthy only as far as the
database administrator is trustworthy, and an audit cannot tell the difference.
BELTAL moves the authoritative copy of those records onto smart contracts, keeps
PostgreSQL only as a fast cache, encrypts all personal and classified detail to
IPFS so nothing sensitive is ever public, and gives auditors a tool that
recomputes each record from the chain and reports whether the database still
matches. Roles, clearance tiers and SBU boundaries are enforced in the contracts
as well as the API, custody tokens are soulbound so equipment cannot be quietly
reassigned, and no single person can both request and approve a transfer of
classified hardware. The result is a system where a doctored record is not just
against policy — it is detectable.
